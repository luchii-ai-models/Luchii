# Frasberg engines - third-party model notices

Frasberg engines are served under the Frasberg brand and are built on these open-weight models.
Each licence lets you rebrand and use the model commercially, subject to its terms:

| Frasberg engine | Built on | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Frasberg Motion Free | LTX-Video 2B (Lightricks) | LTX-Video Open Weights licence |
| Frasberg Motion Fast | LTX-Video 0.9.7 distilled (Lightricks) | LTX-Video Open Weights licence (check the revenue threshold for commercial use) |
| Frasberg Motion Pro | Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B (Wan-AI / Alibaba) | Apache-2.0 |
| Frasberg Motion Ultra | HunyuanVideo / HunyuanVideo-I2V (Tencent) | Tencent Hunyuan Community Licence (not licensed for use in the EU, UK or South Korea; attribution required) |
| Frasberg Image | FLUX.1-schnell (Black Forest Labs) | Apache-2.0 |

Closed models such as Kling (Kuaishou) and Veo (Google) have never released their weights, so they can't be self-hosted or cloned.
