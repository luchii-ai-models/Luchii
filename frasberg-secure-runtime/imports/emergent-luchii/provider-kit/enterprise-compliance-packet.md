# FrasbergAI Enterprise Compliance Packet
Version 1.0 — August 2026

## 1. Certifications & Frameworks
- GDPR compliant · CCPA compliant
- SOC2 (in progress) · HIPAA (optional enterprise add-on) · ISO 27001 (roadmap)

- No prompt retention for training · No output retention for training

- Memory scrubbing after inference

## 5. Audit Controls

## 6. Governance Controls
