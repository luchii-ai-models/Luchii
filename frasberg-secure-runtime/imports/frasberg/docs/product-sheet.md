# Frasberg — One Platform to Run Your Business

Frasberg replaces dozens of point tools with one unified system that automates end-to-end workflows across HR, IT, Finance, Product and more.

---

## Why Frasberg
- Cut software spend and administrative overhead by consolidating tools.
- Automate entire workflows — not just tasks — from onboarding to payroll to device provisioning.
- Share platform capabilities (automation, reporting, permissions) across all apps to scale consistently.

---

## Core Capabilities
- Full‑stack automation: onboarding, payroll, performance, devices, corporate cards.
- Platform‑wide context: generate reports and take action across HR/IT/Finance.
- App Studio: drag‑and‑drop builder to create tailored internal apps and automations.
- Governance & compliance: centralized permissions, immutable audit trail, SOC 2 / ISO-ready.

---

## Teams & Use Cases
- Employee data & compliance: license tracking, equity, immigration pipeline.
- Finance & operations: purchase order automation, vendor onboarding.
- Payroll & compensation: weighted overtime, commission calculators.
- Productivity & internal systems: help desk, product launches, policy notifications.
- Marketing & sales: campaign trackers, lead capture, asset approvals.
- Career development: mentorship programs, reimbursement workflows.
- Global operations: flextime, leave purchases, cross-border tax compliance.
